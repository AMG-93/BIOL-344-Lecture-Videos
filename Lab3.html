Lab 3: Plant Evolution

This lab traces the evolutionary history of plants, from the first algae that colonized land to the diversification of seed plants and flowering plants. Students will examine key adaptations — vascular tissue, seeds, and flowers — that allowed plants to survive and diversify on land, using specimens and phylogenetic comparisons to trace these transitions.
